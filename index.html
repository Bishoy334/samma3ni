Samma3ni سمّعني

      How to play

      • Tap the record to hear 0.1 seconds of a song.
      • Type the song or artist, in English, Franco or Arabic, and pick it from the list.
      • Every wrong guess or skip unlocks a longer clip: 0.5, then 1, 3, 7 and 15 seconds.
      • After 2 misses you pick one of three small hints. After 4 you pick one of three bigger ones. They land as cards next to the record.
      • Each genre has one daily song, the same for everyone. The loop button at the top switches to free play, which never ends.
